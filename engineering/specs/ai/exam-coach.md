# Exam coach

The school records a past paper and memo with its framework, subject, grade,
year, paper code, topic shares and a concrete licence/permission attestation.
Nothing is downloaded or labelled licensed automatically. An allocated teacher
can prepare a version for their current groups; publishing requires the exact
revision, confirmed rights and reviewed question/memo data. Published versions
are immutable; replacement/withdrawal retains the audit record and revokes old
learner reads. Memo bodies remain staff-only.

An approved current Student can read published paper versions in their own
dated subject group and generate a study plan counted back from their published
exam timetable. A released-results projection is not an upcoming exam. Plans
pin the exam date and exact paper versions. Published topic references and
family-permitted mastery evidence determine weighting; the mastery module being
off never authorizes raw staff marks. Schedule changes or withdrawn sources
require a fresh plan, with the old receipt retained for audit.

Answer-photo feedback is a separate follow-on domain/API/client increment. It
will reuse bounded Files scanning, explicit cropped working approval, the
registered vision gateway and safeguarding media/retention contracts. Only the
selected question and its approved memo enter context. Suggested marks and the
model's confidence are provisional; staff explicitly confirm or correct them.
No coaching action writes official assessment marks automatically.

All writes use immutable operation UUIDs and fresh authorization before replay,
inside the tenant lock and after the action. School/account, current enrolment,
guardian approval, role/module withdrawal and source changes are tested. Question papers and staff memos render privately as PDFs from encrypted reviewed definitions; no public or persistent PDF cache is created.
No public file URLs, paid provider activation or invented live-model acceptance
is part of implementation.

Current work: library/study-plan domain is implemented and domain-tested. API/SDK,
staff/learner clients and vision feedback are not yet implemented or qualified.
