# Discipline panel workflows

The school discipline workbench exposes the reviewed discipline services through private panel forms. School managers edit named sanctions, ordered levels and infractions using repeatable labelled rows. There are no punitive defaults. The final threshold is a principal hearing; a separate reasoned principal decision never automatically expels a learner.

Current allocated teachers record factual family-facing descriptions and separate private follow-up notes. Dated grade heads and academic managers review or overturn retained entries with reasons; only an appointed principal records a hearing decision. Old revisions remain readable to authorized staff. Conflicting writes retain an escaped direct-response draft alongside the latest saved revision and require explicit comparison before a new UUID can apply. Private text is never session-flashed or placed in a query. Expected school/account checks and the private historical-year guard run before reflecting a draft.

Grade heads/managers schedule detention with an explicit school-local date/time, grade, current eligible teacher supervisor, location and capacity. Supervisor discovery and paging bind the selected grade/date. Only confirmed approved detention sources can enter a same-year/grade session. Registers distinguish unknown from observed attendance, permit reasoned head corrections, refuse overturned sources, and retain cancelled sessions and captured attendance. Records, learners, supervisors and sessions have reachable pagination.

The actual browser rehearsal covers the nested framework editor, occurrence creation, detention planning/roster/attendance, escalation, principal no-action hearing and retained cancellation at 390 and 1280 px.
