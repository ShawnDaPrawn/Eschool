# My grade

The current, dated academic grade-head appointment remains the authority; no parallel grade-head account role is introduced. The hub is a switchable staff feature and never grants professional counselling, safeguarding, support-plan or clinical-note access. It shows only the currently appointed grades and rechecks appointment, school membership, platform exclusion and module availability on every request and immutable-operation replay.

The hub brings the discipline review queue, current register follow-up, academic watch and intervention metadata into one grade-scoped screen. Attendance distinguishes unrecorded from absent and keeps canonical register corrections and their reasons/audit. Academic watch uses current academic/attendance evidence rather than private counselling or incident-report counts. Opening an intervention body still requires its existing exact owner/caseload policy. Current appointments may read retained grade records through their existing historical policies; the new hub's working roster explicitly uses the selected current year and date.

Actions reuse the existing discipline review/overturn, register correction and communication boundaries. A message to the appointed grade's parents retains the existing grade-head approval exemption; it does not become school-wide permission or bypass fresh guardian/audience checks. Award nominations use the existing current eligible nomination windows and remain distinct from permanent presentation. Unknown writes retain their original UUID/body until resolved, and background/profile replacement erases rendered private data.

Backend and typed contract publication precede the shared app/panel integration. Verification must cover a current head, ended/other-grade appointment, ordinary teacher, parent/learner, platform operator and school replacement; wrong-grade identifiers must be refused before private bodies load. Phone/desktop layout and actual dedicated/classic Teacher navigation are qualified with the other new staff actions in a combined browser target.
