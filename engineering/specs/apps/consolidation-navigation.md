# Whole-app navigation and accessible Teacher actions

Owner-accepted whole-app findings F5–F7,29 October2026.

Teacher home actions must fit narrow screens and enlarged system text without
clamping, truncating or fixed-height overflow. Use Material button semantics,
theme typography and content-driven equal-height rows; reduce columns as text
grows. Keep every destination reachable by scrolling.

Organize Money by task: Fees, Wallets, Vendors and tickets, Cash and reconciliation,
and Money configuration. Preserve permission/module filtering, search and active
page selection. Reader management must share the destination's current combined
Vendor Finance and card-reader authority. An unused disabled module stays hidden;
retained reader revocation stays reachable while spending is paused.

Fee allocation guidance must explain the actual difference between oldest-due
and tuition-first ordering across all supported charges, and retained held credit.
Add panel navigation/copy keys for each supported language. Afrikaans/isiZulu copy
is supplied; fluent acceptance remains external. Legacy fee removal is the next
separate cutover; grouping its current links does not declare it completed.
