# Verified original-parent wallet deposits

Increment8C2 records already-paid cash/EFT claims separately from school-owned
master funding. A current verified Parent who is not a current Student submits
an own-wallet claim. It opens a zero-balance wallet if needed and creates no
posting, receipt identity or optimistic credit. The school explicitly enables
cash and/or EFT for parent deposits; both may be paused without disabling internal
transfers or changing master-funding policy.

A different current Finance operator verifies actual receipt, original holder,
amount, date and reference. Correcting mistaken claim details retains the original.
The verified deposit debits the actual cash/bank asset and credits the original
parent wallet, never the school master. Already received money can be attributed
to that existing wallet after the holder leaves. Cash retains the verifying
custodian. Provider, collector, zero fee, gross/net and EFT settlement reference
are recorded without executing an outside payment. Shared incoming receipt identity
prevents duplicate fee/master/parent booking, including concurrent verification.

Immutable school-owned claims, verified deposits and one terminal action retain
intent and outcome. SQL requires original ownership, independent verifier, current
method policy, exact balanced two-posting source, receipt registration and cash/
settlement metadata. Retained history refuses rollback. Reconciliation detects
orphaned, mismatched or unattributed entries and blocks repair from invalid history.

Parents may withdraw a pending claim; Finance can reject with a safe outcome and
encrypted private note. Neither moves bank or wallet money. Proof is encrypted,
hidden from serialization and excluded from parent APIs, printable receipts,
audit payloads and validation old input. Fresh tenant, actor, role, owner and module
authority apply before permanent replay. Disabled wallets retain readable history.

Additive v2 index/write/cancel contracts and generated Dart SDK feed a collapsed
shared Material3 Parent section with reviewed forms, requested/actual receipts,
cancellation and pagination. Unknown outcomes persist only a school/actor UUID;
the permanent resolver closes uncommitted keys or reports committed without a
duplicate posting. Refresh clears prior private views before revalidating authority.
Learner/Teacher/Facilitator wallets gain no Parent deposit capability.

Gateways, outside payment execution and production activation are excluded.
Withdrawals, cash-up, commerce and6D payer/late-charge work remain. Language
catalogues include draft core Afrikaans/isiZulu/Arabic and explicit English
fallbacks; fluent review remains separate acceptance evidence.
