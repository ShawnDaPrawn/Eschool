# Reserved and verified manual fee refunds

Finance reviews only the original holder's unallocated receipt credit, including
its learner restriction. The school explicitly chooses allowed cash/EFT methods
and independent approval. Approval reserves credit in a separate refund liability
before any outside payout. Allocation and another refund cannot spend those funds.
Finance records an actually verified payout to the original holder, or cancels the
hold back to the original restricted credit. No bank or gateway call is made.

Append-only request/action records retain review, original owner/currency/payment,
actors, exact ledger entries and encrypted evidence. Legal transitions, exact
postings, original receipt budgets and unique payout references are enforced.
Fresh Finance/approver authority, writable fees, current credit and status are
checked under the school lock before actions and permanent replay. Original
invoices, receipts and allocations are never edited.

Statements, allocation and reconciliation include reserved/paid refund cents;
cancelled holds restore available original credit. Parents see only own generic
state and amounts. OpenAPI/SDK/shared fee screens and the Finance workbench are
updated together. Accounting exports retain exact balanced reservation, payout and
cancellation journals. Clearing and due-to-school remain zero. Outside payment
requires Finance's verified evidence and supported cash/bank books; no opening
balance is fabricated. Gateway integration and production activation remain deferred.
