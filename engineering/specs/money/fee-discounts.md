# Approved fee reductions

Finance configures dated, currency-specific sibling, staff, early-settlement,
bursary and exemption rules. The school explicitly chooses whether a separate
approver is required and whether other discounts are excluded or the remaining
charge is the basis. No stacking, percentage, threshold or entitlement is guessed.
Individual bursaries/exemptions name a learner; private evidence remains Finance-only.

The calculation uses canonical dated enrolment, current family roster, current
guardian memberships in explicitly selected school staff roles or actual payments received by an explicit deadline.
A reviewed application shows original/current charge and exact reduction across
original invoice-holder shares. Approval and posting recheck inputs under the
school lock. Every rule/learner/year applies once; overlapping rules obey the
school's choice. Paid reductions return held credit to original restricted
receipts, never a guessed refund. All child credits and the application seal are
atomic, durable, audited and permanently replayable.

Original invoices are retained. Linked credit notes reuse the existing credit
service, including cumulative original VAT, unpaid-first reduction and reconciliation.
Parent statements show only the generic fee-reduction category and own amounts;
private rule/evidence/other payer data are not exposed. Annual bill runs remain
valid gross invoices; Finance reviews the net reduction alongside those originals.
Finance can configure and apply rules from a Material 3 workbench with read-only
history when fees are disabled. Missing eligible source invoices are an explicit
exception rather than a fabricated negative or zero invoice.

Tenant, wrong-role, current rights, independent approver, stale review, exact cents,
rollback, replay and original invoice/VAT retention are required tests. Gateways
and production activation remain outside scope.

Disabling fees retains parent agreements and reduction rules even before the first
ledger posting. The module remains read-only for that history and refuses writes.
