# Adult wallet withdrawals

Increment9 reserves original available wallet funds for a current Parent, Teacher
or Facilitator. A current Student, including a mixed Student/adult profile, cannot
withdraw. Parents must have verified accounts. Own requests never select a third
party wallet. Finance can prepare an explicitly verified return from an existing
departed adult's wallet; it never redirects that liability to the school master.

The school explicitly chooses which method includes exactly R250. Lower rand
amounts use cash and higher amounts bank EFT. Other currencies require an explicit
school threshold. Cash pickup places are published in school time with bounded
capacity. Closing a slot stops new bookings while retaining existing appointments.
Cash threshold configuration retains the wallet currency. Pausing new requests
does not prevent Finance from resolving existing reservations.

A request atomically debits the original wallet and credits its separate pending
liability. A different Finance operator approves the original holder and pickup/
bank instructions. Approval does not execute payment. The holder can cancel before
approval. Once approved, Finance handles cancellation and confirms no outside
payment occurred, protecting the interval while actual payment is being processed.
Rejection/cancellation returns only the original wallet. Verified payment clears
the reservation against an adequately funded cash/bank asset and records actual
paid date, original holder, operator, private evidence and outside payout identity.

Append-only roots/actions, exact balanced postings, legal transitions, independent
approval, slot capacity and outside identities are guarded in SQL and under the
common school lock. Permanent actor-bound operation receipts and opaque app UUIDs
protect retries and interrupted requests. Shared outgoing identity prevents one
cash/bank payout being booked through both fee refunds and wallet withdrawals.
Existing immutable fee-refund payouts are verified before migration DDL and indexed
without changing their original money or proof. Retained history refuses rollback.

Bank instructions, offboarding reasons and payout proof are encrypted and absent
from personal history, print receipts, validation old input and app pending storage.
The Finance panel provides policy, slots, queue/review, approval, actual paid,
release and printable receipts. Additive v2 contracts and generated SDK support
shared family/Teacher/Facilitator request/status/cancel screens. Read-only wallets
retain history. Domain reconciliation checks original roots, reservations, terminal
postings, outside identities and orphans before allowing projection repair.

No gateway, bank call, provider spend or production release is enabled. Cash
custodian evidence is retained for operator cash-up. Vendor work proceeds
independently while final withdrawal app and hosted qualification runs.
