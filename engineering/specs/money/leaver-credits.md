# Reviewed later leaver credits

Finance selects an original tuition invoice and learner with a confirmed ended,
continuous school enrolment. Class moves are continuous attendance; gaps, overlapping
or unconfirmed records require correction/review. The calculator uses the original
annual rate, original policy/period and payer attribution. A later rate or school
policy change never rewrites that contract. Prior policyless ledger invoices use
an explicitly selected school fallback shown in the review.

The school explicitly chooses how previous fee reductions affect departure:
retain their full credit or proportion those reductions to served tuition. Other
billing corrections stay credited. Every original holder's own credits are retained;
benefits are never moved to another payer. Prior leaver credits are counted separately
so repeating an unchanged departure cannot create another refund. An earlier corrected
departure can credit only the remaining legitimate difference. No debt is resurrected.

An immutable reviewed/approved run freezes exact original-holder credit notes.
Independent approval is an explicit school choice. Posting rechecks source dates,
policy, current paid/unpaid shares and permissions under the school lock, and all
credits/receipts/audit roll back on failure. Paid credits restore original restricted
receipt credit. A cash/bank refund is a later separate operation. Parents reuse the
existing own generic leaver-credit statement without private Finance evidence.

SQL also preserves exact encrypted review evidence and original creation instants
when approval states change. The upgrade applies that protection to previously
created reduction reviews without changing their stored evidence or amounts.
