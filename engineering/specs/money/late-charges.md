# Explicit one-off late charges — remaining6D

Late charges start disabled. Finance configures a school-year/currency policy,
choosing annual tuition only or all original charges; flat gross cents or a rate
on recorded overdue debt after allocations; a maximum gross charge; grace days,
new due days and explicit VAT classification/rate. Amounts and caps include VAT.
No current item price, automatic batch or payment provider is used. Held receipt
credit stays separate until explicitly allocated under the school's allocation
process; the review shows that credit alongside the exact overdue basis.

Finance previews and confirms one charge per original learner invoice-line/payer
share. All current unpaid descendants of payer corrections are grouped back to
that original debt, preventing a correction from creating a second late-fee
opportunity for the same payer. Neither original late charges nor their corrected
descendants can generate further late charges. Dates, allocations, prior credits,
policy revisions and current configured payer/guardian authority are rechecked
under the existing school lock. Changed inputs invalidate review.

New policies retain encrypted immutable revisions; late occurrences bind their
exact policy/source/basis and sealed invoice. Existing statement/allocation/credit,
original VAT, integer cents and reconciliation semantics are reused. Finance has
private policy/review/history screens; compatible Parent statements show the
resulting invoice without private reasons or another payer's records. Paused fees
retain policy/invoice history and refuse new posting. No academic access lock.
