# Vendor settlements and collection reports

Increment13 settles collected, previously unbatched wallet orders by vendor and
school-local reporting period. Finance explicitly reviews eligible orders and
original seller/refund/chargeback/settlement terms. A sealed immutable batch
reserves those original order identities exactly once. Pending or cancelled orders
are ineligible; the explicit vendor settlement delay applies to collection time.
No preparation or approval moves money. Another current Finance operator approves
the batch before its final posting. Prepared batches retain the original obligation
until settled; changing a vendor's details never rewrites their bank/terms snapshot.

School-run settlement recognises original net sales and VAT separately, without
an outside payout. Independent vendor settlement records only an already completed,
verified cash/EFT payout to the original merchant; shared external disbursement
identity prevents reusing a fee refund, wallet withdrawal or vendor payout. Cash
custody and actual payment date/evidence are retained. Original funded wallet cash/
bank assets must reconcile and cover the payout; unavailable assets are not invented.

The statement distinguishes original gross, VAT-exclusive sales net, collector fee
and settlement payout. NavigateEd does not collect: collector=school, collection
fee=0 and payable gross=payout. Clearing/due-to-school accounts are opened at zero
for used vendor currencies; the posting kernel continues to refuse Phase6 accounts.
Vendor accounts see only their assigned counter's redacted batches/outcomes. Finance
gets private period/settlement reports. No gateway or automated bank execution.
