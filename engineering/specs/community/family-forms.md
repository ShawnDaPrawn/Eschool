# Family health, consent, meetings and early collection

Reuse the encrypted learner profile Health category for health forms. Fresh current
school membership, account readiness and active guardian rights precede every
private value; platform identities and impersonation cannot enter these readers.
Staff need explicit health access in addition to normal profile-field authority.
Health values never become consent text, meeting notes, push/email payloads or
scan-wide metadata.

Activity/trip consent belongs to a school/year and an actually authorized group.
Freeze the exact consent wording, event dates and named learner audience when
published; editing wording creates a new request. A guardian responds for one
current approved child after reading that exact fingerprint, explicitly attesting
their account name. Append the decision, guardian, timestamp, fingerprint and
server-authenticated attestation; retain previous decisions. This is an auditable
online consent record, not a claim of a qualified legal electronic signature.

Current group membership, enrolment, guardian rights, approval and source dates
are checked again before every response, read, replay and operational decision.
A withdrawn approval or transfer cannot leave an effective consent behind. One
school lock serializes conflicting changes and exact operation IDs deduplicate
transport retries; no body or guardian signature enters audit/outbox payloads.

Parents' evening slots are school-scoped dated teacher appointments, with atomic
booking/cancellation, staff/learner/guardian overlap checks and current allocation
rights. Booking descriptions must not become a clinical record.

Early collection requires a guardian request, explicit school approval, named
collector and bounded collection window. The gate sees an approved current pass
only after an authorized accepted scan for that learner. Confirming the collector
is an explicit human action, never inferred from possessing a QR, and collection
is once-only/idempotent. Current approval, guardian, card/pass and reader authority
are rechecked before release or receipt replay.

Deliver the consent domain first, then typed API/panel/apps. Meetings and gate
passes are independent follow-ons; they are not implemented by this specification.


The staff consent workbench resolves eligible participants on the server for the
complete school-local event span. Authoring remains usable on phones; native
managed readers consume the existing typed roster rather than duplicating an
unrestricted learner picker. Parent signing displays exact immutable wording,
account-name attestation and the separate response deadline. Operational staff
read current effective permission separately from the retained last answer.


Configured Health attachments use private family-purpose wrappers over encrypted
profile files, with current learner/guardian/module and field scope before every
stage. The upload scans source content, strips image metadata, scans sanitized
pixels, and refuses unavailable safety checks. A final authorization failure
removes uncommitted ciphertext. Binary reads validate the current file reference
and purpose again after decryption; a generic profile file is not automatically a
Health attachment. A read-only Health schema exposes permitted values without
editable controls.
