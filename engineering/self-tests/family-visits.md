# Parent meetings and early-collection backend

Implemented: separately switchable `family_visits` module, five tenant-owned tables, encrypted appointment/pass fields, append-only domain audit events and encrypted exact-operation receipts; manager-owned slots, current verified guardian booking/unbooking, scoped host appointments, collision checks and revisioned school collection rules. Parent requests remain pending until another authorised staff member explicitly approves them. Cancellation preserves history and remains available after module shutdown.

The existing delegated online card reader now checks current approved collection passes after a fresh accepted learner QR/SUN scan. It shows only the named collector and time, requires an in-person collector confirmation, and records collection atomically. A matching same-device recent online scan is required; offline/classroom identity records cannot authorise collection. Every lookup/receipt retry refreshes card, delegation, enrolment, guardian approval and staff authority. Collector details clear with portrait expiry/background; a delayed response cannot paint beside a newer scan. An uncertain collection retains only its original opaque operation/scan references for explicit exact retry. No automatic retry occurs.
