The working roster is sorted by current academic/attendance evidence; clinical and confidential incident inputs are excluded. Missing attendance remains unrecorded, and canonical entries must match the learner's dated homeroom. Optional discipline, risk and award sources remain independently unavailable rather than blocking the hub.

Pending discipline items contain only current queue metadata; opening/deciding a record, correcting a register, messaging parents or nominating an award still uses its existing exact policy and audit. The server feature flag is recomputed from current appointments rather than a parallel account role.
