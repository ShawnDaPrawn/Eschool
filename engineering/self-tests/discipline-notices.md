# Discipline notice verification

Implemented generic family updates, grade review requests, principal hearing requests, staff updates and supervised detention updates. Durable UUID intents bind one recipient to a record revision and optional session revision. Duplicate event handling creates no second intent, notice or fanout event. Obsolete revisions disappear from counts/rows and cannot dispatch; future source changes produce a separate immutable intent.

The six focused scenarios pass **6 tests, 53 assertions**. They cover exact replay, generic payloads, source revision changes, current grade-head/principal appointments, guardian/approval withdrawal, a supervisor without ordinary access to the underlying discipline body, detention cancellation/revision, prepared-query allocation and role withdrawal, forged links, module/platform/wrong-school refusal, listener context restoration and transaction rollback. The existing discipline domain/API, staff inbox, school-owned table inventory and actual migration rollback/backfill cohort passes **34 tests, 1,508 assertions**. Complete configured PHPStan passes with no errors; changed-file Pint passes.

Source bodies, names, infraction/sanction labels, locations and private reasons never enter notice bodies or delivery payloads. The external payload contains only the fixed app link and opaque intent UUID. Existing reader permissions remain authoritative, including revision-specific guardian acknowledgement and reasoned grade-head decisions. No clinical or confidential incident input is added. Tests use synthetic schools and a fake queue; they do not claim production delivery, physical-device acceptance or a month of operational use. Dedicated-profile navigation is qualified in the next combined staff client increment.
