# Phase 3 decisions and open questions

Proceed concurrently with Phase 4 using feature-specific dependencies, separate
worktrees and reviewable PRs. Reuse dated `AcademicLeadership` for grade heads.
Counselling/support/safeguarding access must have a dedicated fresh capability;
platform access and impersonation do not grant it.

The first inference foundation defaults off. No provider, model, contract,
processing region or live spend is selected by implementing a protocol interface.
Before enabling a feature, record provider no-training terms and POPIA review,
model/token limits, conservative prices, school and learner budgets, moderation,
subject/source authorization, flagged-content retention and safeguarding owner.
The existing US$10/month staging/email ceiling is not an AI-spending allocation.

The first foundation excludes learners and parents until fresh guardian approval,
own-child/allocation checks, learner budgets and feature-specific source policy
exist. Vision waits for verified private scanned media, metadata stripping and
redaction. A signed/private URL alone is not a moderation decision.

Decide configured band thresholds/minimum assessed share, counselling-note roles,
discipline defaults/visibility, approved digest templates per language, paper
licensing, mastery evidence policy and support-plan visibility with the owner.
Safe defaults and explicit versioning may be built while external evidence waits;
do not silently label invented institution policy as approved.

Recorded-observation evaluation validates the scorer and datasets. A full safety
claim needs real provider/model/prompt provenance, reviewed labels, representative
red-team cases, consecutive qualifying runs and safeguarding acceptance. The tiny
foundation fixture is synthetic protocol evidence only.
