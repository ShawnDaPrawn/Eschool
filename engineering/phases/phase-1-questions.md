# Phase 1: open questions and defaults taken

The decisions Phase 1 needs, and the ones that come up while building it. Until the product owner answers, engineering works to the default below. Each default is recorded as a decision record where it shapes code. Phase 0's answers that Phase 1 relies on (the student number format, Q0.5, and the handle age rule, Q0.6) are in [phase-0-questions.md](phase-0-questions.md).

| # | Question | Default taken | Where it lands | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1.1 | Is one verified guardian enough to approve a learner's access | Yes. Any guardian with a verified email and the right to approve can approve alone. Any other such guardian can withdraw the approval, and the learner loses access at once. The school sees who approved and when. | `learner_approvals`, `learner.approved` (T2) | Defaulted |
| Q1.2 | Which default profile fields are locked | The fields the platform itself depends on: names, date of birth, gender and identity or passport number for learners; names, email, mobile number and relationship for guardians; names, email, mobile number and identity number for staff; medical alerts and allergies for learners. A school cannot remove a locked field or change its type, but can reorder it and choose which roles see it within the platform's limits. Every other default field can be hidden or removed. | `profile_field_definitions` seed (T3) | Defaulted |
| Q1.3 | Do schools switch modules on and off themselves | Yes, within their package: a school admin switches the modules the package includes; a module outside the package needs platform staff. Switching off a module that holds money balances makes it read-only instead of hiding it. | `school_modules`, `ModuleGate` (T3), `/saas` packages (T4) | Defaulted |
| Q1.4 | Which notices are mandatory on each portal | Account and security notices (invitations, sign-in links, password resets, approval requests) on every portal; urgent school notices on every portal; for guardians, a child's absence alert. Everything else is optional, on or off by default as its type says. | `config/notification_types.php`, `notification_rules` (T3) | Defaulted |
| Q1.5 | Size and style of the avatar set | Sixteen flat illustrations in the NavigateEd palette, four each for boys, girls, men and women, with a range of skin tones and hair; SVG in `eschool_core`. | `packages/eschool_core/assets/avatars/` (T5) | Defaulted |
