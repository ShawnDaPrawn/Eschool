# Daily practice

Daily practice is an optional school module. Teachers with current subject-group
allocations approve the question bank. Approved learners use their own sets in
the learner app; guardians can review learning progress but cannot answer for them.

Open **Practice question bank** in the Teacher or Facilitator Teacher app. Select
a group, choose **New practice question**, and select a published topic and an
approved school source. Add a decimal answer or two to six answer choices, at
least one accepted answer, a worked explanation and an estimated duration.
Approve the revision when it is ready. Withdraw availability to stop future reads
of that question, including issued online sets. Cached copies expire within their
short offline lease; known access withdrawal removes them sooner.

Learners open **Daily practice**, choose a subject, and start or resume today's
short set. Check each answer to see the worked explanation. Difficulty feedback
helps schedule later reviews; an incorrect answer is scheduled for more practice.
Practice does not alter official assessment marks and has no leaderboard.

Choose **Save for offline** while connected to download an encrypted copy.
Answers saved on the device remain pending until **Sync saved answers** confirms
them with the school. An expired copy hides its question text; its pending answers
can still be synchronized during the short retention period after a fresh access
check. Removing a copy also removes its pending answers. Switching account or
school destroys the old offline key.

If a save outcome is unknown, use **Retry exact operation**. Keep the frozen
operation until it succeeds or the server refuses it. Refresh the bank after a
revision conflict. No automatic AI drafts or live provider activation are included
in the manual question editor.
