# Polls, surveys and elections

School staff open **Activities → Polls, surveys and elections**. The school must
have the consultations module enabled. Your current school, role and audience
control which consultations appear.

A consultation manager chooses the audience, opening/closing times in the school's
timezone, response identity and result visibility. Drafts support single-choice,
multiple-choice and text questions. Nonstatutory elections support candidate
statements. Review the draft before publishing: the audience, questions, anonymity
and schedule are frozen after publication. Close or cancel with a reason; the
configuration history remains available. Statutory governing-body elections need
the later legal-review workflow.

Eligible staff open a ballot and submit once. A recorded response cannot be
changed; the ballot clearly confirms participation. Anonymous answers show no
respondent or submission time. Private results remain hidden until close,
including manager previews. Live result visibility shows only aggregate counts to
the authorized audience. After close, managers can page individual answers;
ordinary voters see aggregate results and no text answers.

Historical school years and read-only modules preserve authorized reads. A school
or account change requires fresh authorization. Private draft/answer fields are
never copied into session old-input flash. Backgrounding a private panel view
erases it and reloads it on return. Named audience search preserves the current
authoring draft.

Shared family/facilitator app readers and scheduled opening notices are being
implemented separately; the panel does not enable those unfinished surfaces.
