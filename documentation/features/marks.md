title: Assessment marks and released results
---

Schools publish their approved task/question definitions for each term and
allocate teachers to dated teaching groups. A teacher records scores for those
learners; blank questions remain incomplete, and absent, excused and catch-up
work carry explicit reasons. The running percentage and coverage are provisional
until the required work and definitions are complete. Future terms do not stop
a ready term being completed.

A grade head reviews marks, records moderation and corrects reviewed scores with
a reason. School-local lock dates prevent further capture. Reload and compare
when a conflict appears, so another person's changes are not overwritten.

Authorized staff preview selected learners before releasing. Official release
needs complete results; a school may explicitly release a provisional running
mark. Families see only the released evidence, even if later corrections are
still being reviewed. Each release keeps its history. Fee status never prevents
access. A rating is separate from the school's promotion decision.

The exact marks API and generated client support the shared capture grid being
delivered next. Existing app result screens receive official releases through
protected exam projections; use assessment capture/release to change them.
School operators can compare weekly sheets with the private reconciliation tool.
Real pilot and staging acceptance are recorded separately from software tests.


## Shared capture screens

Open **Assessment marks** in teacher Home or Settings, then choose a teaching
group, year and term. On a laptop, use Tab between questions and Enter for the
next learner. Paste a rectangular Excel range into a cell; invalid pastes leave
all entries unchanged. Phone layouts use learner cards. Running marks come from
the server. Save commits the original revisions; a conflict keeps your entries
and asks for comparison before an explicit reload.

Authorized staff select learners, preview their results and release official or
clearly provisional marks. Parents and learners open Assessment marks in
Settings to choose a released period. Only their authorized results appear.
Existing exam screens remain available.
