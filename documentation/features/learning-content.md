---
title: Learning content
---

Use **Learning content** to upload material for your current teaching groups.
Choose the school year and teaching context, give the material a title and short
summary, select its groups and curriculum topic tags, and upload a private file.
Topics come from published curriculum plans; an empty topic list does not prevent
you from continuing.

Supported files are PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, plain text, Markdown, WAV, MP3, MP4,
M4A, WebM and OGG, up to 25 MiB. PDFs are limited to 100 pages. Audio/video needs
an audio track and must fit the school's remaining daily transcription allowance,
with a maximum of 30 minutes per attempt. A recording is refused when too long;
it is never silently cut short. The administrator can disable transcription or
change the daily allowance in **School transcription controls**.

A new source is scanned and converted privately. Use **Refresh content status**
to see its progress. An unavailable scanner or converter leaves the material
unpublished. Follow the message, retry after the service is restored, or upload a
corrected source. A quarantined source needs review with the school. The original
file is never a public download link.

When a draft is ready, review its Markdown, correct transcription or extraction
errors, and check its topic tags and teaching groups. **Save reviewed draft**
updates the readable preview. If someone saved another change first, the
comparison page retains your submitted text so you can copy it and compare with
the current version.

Open **Preview publication**, check the body and audience, confirm that you have
reviewed them, then **Publish reviewed content**. A grade head or other authorized
academic leader publishing another author's material must record a reason.
Learner access depends on current group membership and compatible enrolment;
being selected in a preview does not override those rules.

Published versions cannot be overwritten. Saving an edit or replacing a source
creates a separate draft; the current publication stays available until the new
draft is explicitly published. Open **Private version history** to inspect earlier
versions. **Withdraw published material** removes an item from new learner library
reads while retaining its private history. Author access is rechecked against
current school roles and dated allocations on every action.
