# Refunding original receipt credit

Open **Money → Fee refunds** and choose the school's permitted cash/EFT methods
and separate approval rule. Load the original receipt, enter the amount and
private explanation, and review the original holder and receipt restriction.
Only available receipt credit can be refunded. For a paid invoice, first correct
the charge through a credit note; its original payer retains the restored funds.

**Approve and reserve refund** protects that credit from allocation and another
refund. Arrange and verify the actual cash/EFT payment separately. Record its
unique payout reference, original-holder verification and private evidence. If no
payout occurred, cancel the reservation to restore the original receipt credit.
The app does not execute a bank payment. Keep outside evidence with Finance.

Parents see **Reserved for refund** and **Refunded to you** on their own receipts.
Original receipt amounts and learner restrictions remain unchanged. Fees disabled
means read-only history; refunds do not affect a learner's academic access.
