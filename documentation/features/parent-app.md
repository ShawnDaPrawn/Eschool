# Parents: children, approvals and settings

This page is for parents and guardians using the NavigateEd app on a phone, or the portal at **https://app.navigateed.co.za** in a browser. It covers switching between your children, approving a child's account, accepting an invitation from the school, and the settings for notifications, your school's details and your profile picture. Teachers have the same notification settings, My school and profile picture in the teacher app.

Some of these screens arrive as your school's NavigateEd server is updated. Until your school has them, their entries are simply not shown in **Settings**.

## Switch between your children

When you have more than one child at the school, every screen about a child (assignments, attendance, timetable, results, fees and the rest) shows one child at a time.

1. On **Home**, tap a child's card to open that child's screens. The card shows the child's school.
2. To see another child, tap the bar at the top of the screen with your child's picture and name, then pick a child from the list. In a wide browser window the list of your children stays on the left of the screen; tap a name there.
3. The screen you are on changes to the child you picked, so you can compare, for example, two children's timetables without going back to Home.

The app remembers the child you last picked, also after you close it. With one child there is nothing to switch, so the bar is not shown.

## If your children are at different schools

When you are linked to more than one school, the school's crest and a **Switch school** button appear at the top of **Home**.

- Tap it and pick a school. The app opens **Home** again with that school's information.
- Picking a child who is at another school switches to that school for you.

If all your children are at one school, you will not see the button.

## Approve your child's account

A school does not switch on a learner's own account until a parent or guardian has approved it. When a child is waiting for you:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Approvals**. Children under **Waiting for your approval** are listed first, then those you have **Approved**.
2. Tap **Review and approve** next to the child.
3. Read what you are agreeing to, then tap **I agree, approve**.

You can change your mind later: open **Approvals** again, tap **Withdraw approval** next to the child and confirm. Your child is then signed out and cannot sign in until you approve again.

## Accept an invitation from the school

A school can invite you with a link to `app.navigateed.co.za/invite/...`.

1. Open the link on your phone (it opens the app if you have it, otherwise the portal in your browser).
2. Check the school and the child or children named in the invitation.
3. If you are asked to, create a password (at least 8 characters) and type it again. Tap **Accept and sign in**.

You are signed in as a parent of those children. An invitation link works once and only until the date it shows; if the app says the invitation has expired or was already used, ask the school for a new one.

## Choose which notifications you get

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notification settings**. You can also tap the gear at the top of the **Notifications** screen.
2. Each kind of notice is listed with a switch for each way the school can reach you: **Push** (on your phone), **Email** and **In the app**.
3. Turn a switch off to stop that kind of notice reaching you that way. The change is saved at once.

Some notices show a lock: your school always sends them, and its reason is shown under the switch. They cannot be turned off.

## My school

Open **Settings**, then **My school** to see the school's details:

- **Contact the school:** tap the phone number to call, the email address to write, or the website to open it. The address is shown too.
- **Documents:** the school's documents, such as the fee schedule or the code of conduct. Tap one to open it.
- **Term dates:** when each term starts and ends.

If your children are at different schools, pick the school at the top of the screen. When a school has not added its details yet, the screen says so.

## Your profile picture

1. Open **Settings**, then **Profile picture**.
2. Pick one of the avatars, grouped as boys, girls, men and women. It is saved straight away.
3. Or tap **Upload a photo** and choose a photo. The school checks photos first; until it has, the screen shows "Your photo is waiting for the school to approve it."

## Links that open the app

Links from the school to `app.navigateed.co.za` (for example to **My school** or **Notifications**) open the matching screen in the app on your phone, or in the portal in a browser. You may be asked to sign in first. Tapping a notification works the same way, and switches to the right school first when the notice is about another of your schools.
