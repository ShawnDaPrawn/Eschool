---
title: Curriculum and assessment plans
---

Open **Academics → Curriculum plans**. Teachers see plans for their allocated
subject, grade and year. School publishers see the school's versions.

1. Start a draft and choose its subject, grade and school year.
2. Add a term, topics and optional week ranges.
3. Set the term's components and their percentages, then add weighted tasks.
4. Enter exact maximum marks, due dates and optional question maxima/topic links.
5. Save. A publisher can publish the prepared term without completing future terms.

Included component percentages and task percentages within each component must
total 100% before publication. Question maxima must total the task maximum. Term
and annual weights are separate; use the school's reviewed subject policy. An
incomplete annual policy does not prevent drafting or publishing a ready term.

Published definitions cannot be edited. **Copy to draft** makes a new version;
choose the same school year for a revision or another year for reuse. New-year
carry-forward is an explicit choice and keeps definition history as drafts.
Learner marks and reports are never copied to a new year. Review shifted dates,
term mappings and source versions before publishing.

A stale save or locked term returns a conflict so newer work is preserved. Keep
your draft open to compare your changes with the reloaded version.
