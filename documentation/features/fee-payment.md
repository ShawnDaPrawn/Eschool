## Supported Payment Gateway for Online Fee Payment
- Stripe

Razorpay, Paystack and Flutterwave are no longer supported. Payments made through them earlier still show in the fees transaction logs.

## Fees Configuration

To utilize the fees feature with online payment, administrators must complete this form by providing their Stripe details. Additionally, they need to specify the currency code and symbol for the payments.

## Session Year

Parents can pay their students' fees only for the current session year. Each student can be assigned to a particular class in a session year and a different class in the subsequent session year. In this section, administrators can create new session years and manage existing ones.

When setting up a session year, administrators need to enter its name, starting date, ending date, fees due date, and fees due charges (in percentage). Optionally, administrators can enable installment fee payments and set up one or more installments with names, due dates, and due charges (in percentage).

These dates and charges will be applicable to students paying their fees in the current session year. Administrators can designate the current session year from the general settings in the system configuration.

## Fees Type

Administrators can create various types of fees from this section, such as exam fees, gym fees, sports fees, and more. Each fee type can have a title and description to provide additional context.

## Assign Fees Classes

Administrators can assign different types of fees to specific class sections in this section. These fees can either be optional or non-optional, and their amounts can vary for each class section.

For instance, sports fees could be $90 for Class 9A and $200 for Class 12B. Administrators can customize the amounts for individual fee types according to each class.

## Fees Paid

Administrators can view the fee payment status of all students from various classes in this section. If students and parents are making payments directly to the admin/principal using cash or cheque, the admin can record those payments in the compulsory/optional fee payment section.

Furthermore, administrators have the convenience of downloading PDF receipts for fees that have already been paid.

## Fees Transactions Logs

This section displays logs of all fees transactions. Administrators can access detailed information about each transaction related to fees.

## Fees Online Payment (Parent Login eSchool App)

Parents can conveniently pay their student's current session year fees online through the application. In the student options -> fees section, they can access information about all session years where fees have been paid and download receipts for their records.

Parents have the flexibility to pay pending fees for the current session year. They can either make full payments for compulsory fees or, if installment options are enabled, pay one or more installments. Additionally, they can pay optional fees at any time without concerns about due charges.

In the settings -> fees transaction option, parents can review all fee payment transactions, including the amount and status of each payment made.

## Annual fee plans and enrolment preview

Finance staff with the fee-plan permission can open **Money → Fee plans** to
create an annual rate for all learners, a grade or a class. Enter the amount and
currency, choose the school year and save as Draft or Active. Archived and draft
plans do not match the preview.

Choose the school year and a preview date to check dated enrolments. Missing or
overlapping plans and enrolments that need date confirmation or a pro-rata/refund
rule appear as exceptions. Totals remain separate for each currency. This is an
annual-rate preview; it does not create invoices, charge families or take payments.

To prepare the next year, open a plan, choose **Copy to a new school year**, enter
the uplift percentage and create the draft copy. Review the resulting amount and
scope before activating it.

## Family accounts and annual bill runs

In **Money → Family accounts**, create an account, choose the school year and
learners, then select the guardians responsible for it. Each guardian needs
current fee visibility for every selected learner. Enter shares totalling 100%.
Shares are your explicit school decision; the system does not infer them from
legacy parent fields. Existing invoices keep their recorded holders and amounts.

In **Money → Bill runs**, prepare an annual preview with the enrolment date,
due date and confirmation that these annual tuition fees carry no VAT. Review
every exception and amount. Missing payers, overlapping plans and unresolved
pro-rata/leaver rules need correction before this run can proceed. Previously
invoiced annual tuition is shown and excluded.

Approve the preview, then explicitly post it. Inputs are checked again at each
stage; changed inputs require a fresh preview. Posting creates permanent family
invoices and learner lines; it does not collect payment. Only the current school
year can post. Repeating the same submission does not create another invoice.

Open an account to see its receivable balance and invoice history. Posted
invoices retain their original learner amounts and holder shares. Disabling fees
keeps this financial history read-only. Parent-facing statements, allocations,
credits and additional fee policies follow separately. Payment gateway integration
is deferred and requires no provider to prepare these accounts and bill runs.

## Overdue fee reminders

School staff can enable overdue fee reminders in Fees Configuration and set the
fee reminder start date with the school year's fee settings. The learner profile
can show that fees are overdue once the applicable due date and reminder start
date have passed, including an unpaid installment.

Unpaid fees never restrict learner access to the application, learning, marks or
released reports. Learners sign in to Home, and parents can open their child's
academic screens while fees remain unpaid. Fee statements and payments remain in
the Fees section under the school's module and guardian permissions.

## Student Can Pay Their Own Fees

Admin can enable settings from the Fees Configurations form, which allows students to pay their fees. When the Admin enables the student fee payment mode, it activates the necessary options and features to facilitate fee payments by students.
