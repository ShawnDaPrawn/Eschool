---
title: Mid-year learner changes
---

Open **Academics → Mid-year learner changes** and find the learner by name. Choose
the school year and change type. Enter the date and reason, preview the effect,
then apply the reviewed change. If another officer changes the records first,
preview your draft again.

- **Join:** choose the first day, homeroom and subject groups. You can finish group
  allocations later. Catch-up/excused explains earlier tasks without inventing
  zero scores. Requesting a fee pro-rata review does not post any billing.
- **Leave:** choose the last attended day. Current enrolment and group dates end
  that day, later scheduled entries are cancelled and kept in history, and learner
  app access ends the following day. Parents keep access to their child's released
  records. Use a later explicit join if the learner returns.
- **Subject change:** select the current membership and new group. Grade defaults
  choose catch-up, excused or carried. Carried applies only to existing marks for
  the same subject; a different subject needs catch-up/excused.

An officer with sensitive-export permission can select a departure, released
reports and current permitted documents for a signed exit ZIP. Preview the contents,
queue the copy, then refresh its status. A failed copy with changed sources needs
another preview. Only the requester can download that copy while authorized.
Compare its SHA-256 and signing-key fingerprint through the authenticated school
page before hand-off. A key included inside the ZIP alone is not proof of origin.

Export copies expire after the school's chosen 1–30 days (default 7); links expire
within ten minutes. Downloaded copies remain with their recipient. Educational
records stay retained. A dated join never bypasses verified guardian approval.
