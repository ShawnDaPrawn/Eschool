---
title: School messages
---

Open **Communication → School messages** in the panel. Drafts and authorized
reviews appear beside the message editor on a laptop; the sections stack on a
narrow screen. Legacy notices and direct chat retain their existing screens.

Choose the school year, audience and people. Standard audiences are the whole
school, a grade, homeroom or teaching group; choose its learners or their parents
and guardians. Whole-school messages require an author rule or current school
leadership authority. Teachers see their dated allocations, and grade heads see
their appointed grades. Current physical enrolment determines learner membership.
An ended guardian link overrides an old parent field.

**Saved custom audiences** contain explicit school contacts. Save the list before
changing contacts pages. An owner or communication manager can edit/archive a
list; another author can use it only within their own current contact authority.
A draft copies the list revision and contacts, so editing the list later does not
change an already prepared message. Contacts that left your scope are identified and removed when you save.
An empty list can be archived; an active list needs at least one current contact.
An archived list cannot be selected for a new draft.

Write the subject and Markdown, choose delivery channels and optionally schedule
in the displayed **school timezone**. Leave the time blank for immediate sending.
Headings, lists and safe HTTPS links are supported. Raw HTML and remote images
are removed. Limits are 20,000 characters and 64 KiB. Save before previewing;
unsaved edits disable send and withdrawal actions. If another session changed the
draft, the comparison screen retains your submitted text for copying. Validation
errors restore the subject, Markdown, choices and original revision for the same
account, school and exact draft; save before previewing that restored text.

**Preview saved message** shows the current recipient count, channels, schedule
and approval requirement. **Submit previewed message** requests approval or queues
sending. Ordinary teacher roles require approval by default. Grade heads need
none; current principals and communication managers default to no approval.
**Message author rules** lets a manager override staff role defaults. A sender
cannot approve their own message. Grade heads review only their appointed grades;
a school-wide author grant does not expand that review authority.

A reviewer opens the saved preview, enters a reason and approves or returns the
message for changes. Editing a draft clears its approval. At scheduled delivery,
standard audiences resolve again from the current roster, and sender/approver
rights are checked afresh. If those rights changed or no recipients remain,
the draft reports that delivery needs review; edit and submit it again.

Sending freezes the recipients and message body. Later group/grade changes do not
add historical readers. Readers still need an active account and their current
guardian/learner rights. New guardians do not gain messages sent before they were
recipients. Mandatory school channels can be added automatically; optional channels still
respect school rules and personal preferences.
Notifications contain generic text and an app link, with no private subject/body.
Queued attempt counts are not provider delivery receipts. Read acknowledgements
are separate and shown only in the staff workbench.

**Withdraw message**, with a reason, hides the private reader and stops pending
email/push when their worker checks access. A withdrawn notice cannot recall a
message a person already read. Published text, scopes and channels are immutable;
prepare a new draft for a correction. Polls, surveys and elections follow later.
