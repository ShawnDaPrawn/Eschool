---
title: Languages
---

Both apps offer **Afrikaans** and **isiZulu** in their language settings. The panel
also lists them under its language chooser. English remains the default. The
choice applies to interface labels; a teacher's message, learner name, uploaded
content or school-authored comment remains in its original language.

The shared academic screens use the selected language for mark capture,
registers, timetables, reports and school messages. The new language files are
development drafts pending fluent review and school terminology acceptance.
If a label needs correction, record the screen, language and intended wording
without including learner details or private message text.
