# Choose a handle

Open **Settings → Handle and requests**. Choose a short name with 3–20
lower-case letters, digits, dots or underscores, beginning with a letter.
Avoid your surname, student number and date of birth.

Learners under 18 need guardian approval. Your proposal stays pending until
your verified guardian opens the same settings page and chooses **Approve**.
They can also reject a request. Requests expire after seven days.

You can change a handle once per term. The page shows the next change date.
Your previous handle stays reserved for 90 days. Existing email, GR-number
and student-number sign-ins continue to work.

If your name is unavailable, the page keeps what you typed so you can adjust
it. Guardians must verify their email before reviewing requests.
