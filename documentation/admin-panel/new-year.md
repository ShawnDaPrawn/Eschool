# Start the new school year

**Academics > School year > Start the new school year** prepares next year while this year carries on, then makes it the current year when you are ready. Nothing changes for teachers, parents or learners until the last step, and nothing is copied before then. You need permission to create school years; the last step also needs permission to edit them.

The page has five steps. You can go back and forth with **Back** and **Next**; what you tick is kept in this browser tab if you leave the page and come back.

## 1. Next year

The name, first and last day, fee due date, late charge and terms are filled in from this year, moved on by a year (for example "2026" becomes "2027"). Change anything that is different and select **Create next year**. Leave a term's dates empty to skip it; terms can be changed later under **Terms**.

Next year now exists in the background: it shows in the header's year box, read-only, and this year is still the current year.

## 2. Carry forward

Tick what next year should start with from this year:

- Subjects per grade, with electives
- Homeroom and subject teachers
- Timetable
- Grading scale
- Fees per grade
- Leave settings

Each line says how many will be copied. If next year already has its own (for example you set up its grading scale by hand), nothing is copied for that line. Promotions recorded on **Promote learners** take effect at the switch.

## 3. Year-end checks

Things worth settling before this year closes: exam results published, learners promoted into the new year, leave requests decided, and fees still owed. None of them stops the switch, and this year's records stay readable afterwards.

## 4. Preview

What happens when you make next year current: which items are copied and how many, and how many learners move to their new homerooms or leave the school.

## 5. Make it current

Select **Make 2027 current** (with your new year's name) and confirm. The ticked items are copied, promotions take effect, and next year becomes the current year in the panel and the apps. Home shows the new year.

This year stays in the panel as a read-only past year (see [School years](./session-year.md)). If you switched by mistake, make the old year current again on **School years**.
