# Events and admission tickets

Enable Ticketing after configuring Wallets and Vendors. Finance opens **Money →
Events and admission tickets** and records the event's sale and admission windows
in the school's time zone. Start with a draft while details are being agreed.

Prepare a new vendor product with stock tracking switched off, its exact price
including any VAT, and the unit "admission". Attach it to the event, choose general
or named learner admission, enter the capacity, and explicitly choose whether
transfers are disabled, restricted to current families, or allowed between current
wallet profiles. Activate the event, type and product when ready. Ticket capacity
is independent of physical stock; capacity cannot be reduced below tickets issued.
Keep a private reason for every event or admission-policy revision.

Families and staff buy tickets in **Wallets → Shop and orders**. Named tickets
require an authorised original learner. Ticket purchases use the selected wallet's
balance and spending/category limits. Buy physical goods and tickets in separate
checkouts. Digital tickets are issued immediately; their original order already
records digital fulfilment for vendor settlement.

The holder opens **Admission tickets** to see the event, original order, named
learner where applicable, private QR/typed number and redemption metadata. To
receive a transfer, show the sender your short-lived receiving code. The sender
reviews the intended recipient before confirming. A named ticket keeps its
original learner and may only pass to someone currently authorised for that
learner. A transfer rotates both admission credentials; it moves no wallet money.

The assigned Vendor operator chooses **Event admissions** in the counter panel or
scanner, then scans the admission QR or types its number. Review the event and
named learner, if any, and confirm one actual admission. A replay or recovered
original result is a receipt of that admission: do not admit the ticket again.
An unknown interrupted operation is closed before another confirmation. Old
transferred credentials, other vendors, frozen access and admissions outside the
configured window are refused.

Pausing Ticketing preserves private original sales, ownership and redemption
history. It prevents new purchases, transfers and admissions. No bank gateway,
external payment or production activation is enabled by ticketing.
