# Search

The search box at the top of every page finds people and pages by name. Press `/` or Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) on any page to jump to it.

## What it finds

| Group | Search by | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pages and settings | A page's name or a word about it, such as "terms", "fees" or "school profile" | The page |
| Learners | Name, email or admission number | The learner's report, or the learner list, filtered to them |
| Parents | Name, email or phone | The parent list, filtered to them |
| Teachers | Name or email | The teacher list, filtered to them |
| Staff | Name or email | The staff list, filtered to them |
| Grades and homerooms | A grade or homeroom, such as "Grade 5" | Grades and homerooms |
| Subjects | Name or code | Subjects |

Type at least two letters. Results appear as you type, up to five per group; use the arrow keys to move through them and Enter to open one. **See all results**, or Enter without choosing a result, opens the results page with up to twenty per group.

## What you see depends on your role

Search only shows what you are allowed to open. A teacher finds the learners of their own homerooms and the pages their role allows, not parents, other teachers or settings.

Learners are searched in the school year chosen in the header. If someone is not found, check the spelling, try part of the name, or switch the year in the header to search another year's learners.
