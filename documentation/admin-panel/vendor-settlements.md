title: Vendor settlements
---

Finance opens **Money → Vendor settlements**, selects a vendor and the collection
period, and reviews the original collected orders. Only orders that meet the
merchant's settlement delay and have not already been batched are available.
Follow the original-order links to review sale terms and liability, select the
orders and confirm the merchant terms. Preparation reserves those orders in an
immutable batch. It does not move money.

A different Finance operator reviews and approves the exact batch. The batch
retains the merchant details and payment instructions reviewed at preparation;
changing the catalogue later does not change this record. Prepared batches stay
available until their original obligation is settled. Do not prepare duplicate
outside payouts or substitute a different recipient.

For a school-run vendor, **Record original vendor settlement** recognises the
original sales and VAT separately. It does not pay cash or transfer money outside
the school. For an independent vendor, record only a cash or EFT payout that has
already completed. Verify the original merchant, actual reference, settlement
date and private evidence. The form does not execute a bank payment. Cash records
the operator who paid it. The corresponding wallet cash/bank asset must reconcile
and cover the amount. Wallet backing must also be writable for outside payouts.

After success, check the retained original record before any further action.
Resubmitting the same request cannot pay or post the batch twice. An outside
reference already used for a fee refund, wallet withdrawal or vendor settlement
cannot be recorded again. Zero-value batches create no zero money posting.

The report distinguishes **Original gross**, **VAT-exclusive sales net**, **VAT
included**, **Collector fee** and **Vendor settlement amount**. The school remains
the collector; NavigateEd's collector fee is zero and collection accounts stay at
zero. No payment gateway or Phase 6 collection is activated. Download a private
settlement CSV for the selected vendor/period.

Assigned operators choose **View this counter settlement history** in the Vendor
panel or app to see only their own batches, original order totals and settlement
dates. Buyer identities, wallet IDs, bank instructions and private Finance evidence
are excluded. Pausing commerce keeps authorised history readable and stops writes;
revoking the counter assignment also stops vendor history access.
