# Desk wallet top-ups

Open **Money → Desk wallet top-ups**. Finance first funds the school master wallet
with verified school-owned money. An authorized policy manager sets the amount above
which a second operator must approve. Zero requires a second approval for every grant.

Choose the recipient, enter the amount and a private reason, review and confirm.
A grant at or below the threshold is credited immediately. A larger grant reserves
funds until another authorized operator reviews and approves it. Open its receipt to
print it. Parents and recipients can see its status in their wallet receipts.

A pending school-owned grant can be cancelled with a reason; its reservation returns
to the school master. Credited grants cannot be edited or cancelled through this
screen. Policy changes preserve stricter approval requirements on pending grants.

Desk processing permission does not grant bank-funding or policy access. Disabled
wallets retain read-only history. Parent deposits and transfers use their own flows;
do not record parent-owned money as school-owned master funding.
