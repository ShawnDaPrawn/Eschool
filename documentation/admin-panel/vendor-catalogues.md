# Vendors and stock

Finance opens **Money → Vendors and stock**. Enable the Vendors module and
configure the school's wallet currency first. Catalogue preparation continues
when wallet transactions are paused.

Create a category with a permanent key, then a school-run or independent vendor.
Drafts can be saved while agreements are being prepared. To activate a vendor,
record its legal trading name, VAT registration decision, refund and chargeback
responsibility, settlement method, frequency, delay and agreed terms. Independent
EFT vendors also need private bank instructions. Those instructions are encrypted
and never filled back into the edit form. Leave that field blank to retain them.

Create each product with its permanent SKU and stock mode, unit label, category,
price including VAT and explicit tax treatment. A standard VAT rate uses basis
points: 1500 means 15%. Products sold by a vendor that is not VAT registered use
the corresponding tax choice and rate zero. Review and archive active products
before changing a vendor's VAT registration.

Tracked stock starts at zero. Record counted deliveries, waste or corrections
with a unique physical reference and a private reason. Receipts add units; waste
removes units. A correction can add or remove units. Negative stock and duplicate
deliveries are refused. Archive a product or vendor to hide it from storefronts;
its stock and history remain available to Finance. Disabling Vendors retains
history in read-only mode.

Catalogue setup creates no money and sends no settlement. Orders, storefront
purchases, fulfilment and dedicated vendor operator access are subsequent
increments. No payment gateway is required for this preparation.
