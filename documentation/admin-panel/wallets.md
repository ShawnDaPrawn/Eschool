---
title: Wallets
---

Open **Wallets** in the Money section with wallet finance permission. Configure
your currency and school daily spending maximum. Choose explicitly whether child
wallets require guardian controls. Currency is fixed after the first wallet opens.
A zero cap stops purchases. This setting does not switch off academic access.

Parents, learners, teachers and facilitators use **Wallets** in their app. Opening
a wallet creates a zero balance. A verified guardian with current approval rights
can read a linked child's statement and set daily/category restrictions. Learners
cannot raise their own limits. Finance can read school wallet balances and private
statements. Turning the wallet module off retains history as read-only.

Funding, transfers and purchases are subsequent increments. No payment gateway is
connected by configuring a wallet. Existing invoices and receipts stay unchanged.
