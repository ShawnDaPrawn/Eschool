# School information

Open **Settings → School information** to update the name, short name, crest,
contacts and timezone used in the apps. The editor affects your current school.
Leaving the replacement crest empty keeps the existing picture. Clear an
optional contact field to remove it.

Use **Publish document** for school policies and schedules such as the code of
conduct or fee schedule. Choose a PDF up to 5 MB. Publication creates a public
link. **Unlist document** removes it from My school; shared links still work.

The page shows the current school year's term dates and links to the existing
term editor. In either app, people open **Settings → My school** to find contact
actions, published documents and dates. Guardians can choose between their
children's schools.
