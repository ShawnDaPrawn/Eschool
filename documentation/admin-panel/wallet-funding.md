---
title: School wallet funding
---

Finance uses **School wallet funding** in the Money section to record funds already
received and verified as belonging to the school. Configure the wallet currency and
supported cash/EFT methods first. Enter the amount, unique receipt or bank reference,
received date and private verification evidence. Confirm that these are school-owned
funds. Open the resulting receipt to print it.

A cash receipt or bank transaction may be recorded once across fees and wallets.
Replaying a confirmed submission shows its existing receipt. If the original
submission outcome is uncertain, retry it with the existing form rather than
recording another reference. Currency is fixed after money is recorded.

Parents' money must use the separate parent funding flow. School master funding does
not by itself credit a person's wallet. Finance desk top-ups and parent transfers
are subsequent increments. Switching wallets off preserves read-only history.
