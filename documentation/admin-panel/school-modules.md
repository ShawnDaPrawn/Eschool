# School modules

Open **Settings → School modules** to see the features included in your school
package. Choose **Enable** or **Switch off** beside a feature. Changes apply to
your selected school. Required features, including people and guardian approval,
stay on.

A feature outside your package needs platform staff to change the package.
If another enabled feature depends on one you want to switch off, switch off
the dependent feature first.

Switching off fees keeps existing financial records readable and stops new
changes. It does not erase charges, receipts or transactions. Re-enable fees
to resume changes.

Disabled features disappear from navigation and refreshed app context. Native
apps receive a silent refresh message; browsers refresh when resumed. Old app
versions remain protected by the server's route checks.
