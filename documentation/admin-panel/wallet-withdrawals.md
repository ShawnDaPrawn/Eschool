# Wallet withdrawals

Parents, teachers and facilitators open **Wallets → Adult wallet withdrawals**.
Review the available balance and school cash-or-bank boundary, enter the amount,
then choose a published cash pickup slot or supply private details for your own
bank account. Confirm and submit. The amount is reserved immediately and waits
for Finance approval. Learners cannot withdraw.

You can cancel before Finance approves. After approval, contact Finance before
changing the request: payment may already be in progress. Approval does not send
money. The receipt appears after Finance verifies actual payment. Use Refresh to
see current status. Interrupted requests can be resolved safely after reopening.

Finance configures **Money → Adult wallet withdrawals**, including which method
includes exactly R250, acceptance of new requests and available cash pickup slots.
Closing new bookings retains existing appointments. A different Finance operator
checks the original holder and instructions before approving. Complete the actual
cash/bank payment outside the app, then record its reference, paid date and private
proof. For cash, record the operator who handed it to the holder.

If no outside payment occurred, Finance can release the reservation to its original
wallet. Departed adults' existing money can also be returned through the verified
original-holder process. Neither route moves money to school master funding.
Private banking and verification details are excluded from printable receipts.
Disabled wallets retain readable history. No payment gateway is involved.
