id: vendor-orders
title: Vendor orders and collection
---

Open **Money → Vendor orders** to choose a counter and review its original paid
orders. Enter the order number or scan/paste the complete wallet order QR. Review
the listed goods and confirm handover. A successful validation allows one
collection; an already collected or cancelled order cannot be handed over again.
If a request is interrupted, check the original receipt before repeating handover.

For goods confirmed never handed over, authorised Finance can cancel the original
order with a private reason and explicit checks. This returns the original gross
only to the original wallet and restores its counted stock once. Collected goods
cannot use this cancellation. School/vendor liability and settlement agreements
remain separate from buyer balance controls.

Parents, learners, teachers and facilitators shop through **Wallets → Shop and
orders**. They select an authorised wallet, review quantities and the exact total,
then confirm once. Current guardian approval, school/child category and daily
limits, wallet availability, prices and stock are checked again when paying.
Receipts and collection QR codes appear in **Your orders**. An interrupted money
request requires an outcome check before another purchase; do not start a new
request to guess whether the first succeeded.

When commerce is paused, authorised history remains readable and purchases,
collection and cancellation stop. These are internal wallet purchases; no external
payment gateway is involved. Dedicated vendor login and native scanner access are described in
[Vendor operators and counter access](vendor-access.md).
