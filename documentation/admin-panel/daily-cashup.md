title: Daily cash-up
---

Use **Money → Daily cash-up** to compare actual cash records with the notes and
coins at your desk. The school grants **money-cashup-manage** to its Finance cash
operators. System School Admins receive it by default; other roles need an explicit
grant. Choose the custodian, currency and actual count date.

Opening cash carries earlier actual cash forward. Cash fee receipts, verified
school wallet funding and parent deposits increase custody; completed cash fee
refunds, adult wallet payouts and vendor settlements decrease it. EFT, wallet
grants, transfers and purchases are excluded. Original receipt/payout dates are
used where recorded; fee refunds show their payout recording date explicitly.

Enter the actual total counted, private evidence and confirmation. Explain every
difference from the report. Corrections also require a reason. The original count,
its report and each later revision remain available. Recording a shortage or
surplus never adjusts the ledger or creates money. Resolve the physical difference
through the school's actual cash process, retaining the evidence.

A later original receipt or payout may change a past report. Review the notice,
recount the cash and retain a corrected revision. A stale form cannot replace a
newer report or count. Replaying the same request returns the original count once.
Unattributed cash postings or incorrect cached balances require reconciliation
before another count can be recorded.

Counts and private history remain available while the money modules are paused.
Only the signed-in operator can record their own count; authorised Finance can
review other recorded custodians. The CSV contains totals and original record IDs,
with private count evidence and explanations excluded. Recipient-confirmed custody
handovers and cash-payout availability controls follow in the next increment10B.
