# One-off late charges

Finance opens **Money → One-off late charges**. Posting requires fee-billing
permission; configuring the rule requires fee-plan permission. No late charge is
available until the school saves and enables its own year/currency rule.

Choose annual tuition only or all original charges, a flat gross amount or a rate
on unpaid overdue debt after allocations, a maximum gross amount, grace days,
days until the new invoice is due and VAT treatment. Amounts and caps include VAT.
Leave the unused flat amount or percentage empty. A rule change retains its
original revision and requires a new posting review.

Open a family account and select an original overdue learner debt and payer.
Review the recorded unpaid basis, separate held receipt credit, exact charge,
VAT and new due date. Add the private reason and confirm once. Held credit is
shown separately; allocate it through the normal payment workspace when needed.
The rule does not allocate receipts or post charges automatically.

Only one late charge is permitted for each original learner invoice-line/payer.
Unpaid payer corrections are traced back to that original debt. Late invoices and
their corrected descendants never create additional late charges. A payment,
policy or authority change requires a fresh review. Parents see their own late
invoice in the existing statement without private Finance reasons or another
payer's debt. Existing posted history is retained; adjustments use the normal
credit process. Pausing fees preserves history and prevents new posting.
