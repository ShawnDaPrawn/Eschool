# Correct an unpaid payer share

Finance opens **Money → Unpaid payer corrections**. This requires both fee-credit
and bill-posting authority. Choose the original family account and unpaid invoice
share. Select another explicitly configured payer on that account, enter the
unpaid amount to correct and a private explanation.

The recipient must currently be an active Parent with fee visibility for the
original learner. Configure future family-account shares separately; this workflow
corrects the selected historical unpaid debt within the original account. It does
not change the learner, future payer roster or original invoice.

Review the original invoice, learner, original and intended payers, exact amount,
original VAT and due date before confirming. The action records an unpaid credit
for the original payer and an equal replacement invoice for the intended payer.
School balances and income do not change. Current item prices, current VAT rates
and another family's paid money are never substituted for the original amounts.

Paid receipts, allocations and held credit stay with their original payer. A paid
share cannot be corrected through this action; use the separate reviewed credit
or refund workflow where appropriate. If a payment, source debt or payer authority
changes during review, reopen the unpaid share and review it again. Retry the
original confirmation after an interrupted response rather than preparing another
correction for the same intent.

Each parent sees only their own resulting debt and credit. Private Finance reasons
and another payer's details are excluded from parent statements. Pausing Fees
retains correction history and refuses new postings. This does not affect learner
academic access or enable a payment gateway.
