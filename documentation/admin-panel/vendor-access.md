title: Vendor operators and counter access
---

Finance opens **Money → Vendor operators** to create a dedicated operator identity.
Verify the actual person, supply a temporary password securely and assign only
their counters. Do not reuse a staff or family account. Revoke an assignment as
soon as access ends; revocation remains available while commerce is paused.

Operators open **/vendor-counter/sign-in** or the **NavigateEd Vendor** app flavour.
Use the school code and dedicated identity supplied by Finance. Change the initial
password and read and accept current terms when prompted. Select an assigned
counter to see its ready, collected or cancelled orders.

Scan the wallet order QR or enter its order number. Review the original goods,
quantities and total, then confirm one handover. Give the listed goods once after
successful validation. Lookup alone does not authorize supplying goods. If camera
access is unavailable, enter the order number or paste its QR code.

If the app reports an interrupted request, choose **Check handover outcome** before
another confirmation. A recovered or replayed collection is already recorded:
check the counter record and do not hand over goods a second time. Collected and
cancelled orders cannot be collected again. Read-only counters retain receipts
but cannot validate handovers. Sign out when leaving the counter.

Vendor accounts show assigned order information only. Finance controls refunds,
operator assignments and financial records. See [Vendor settlements](vendor-settlements.md) for private batch history. No
external payment gateway is involved.
