# Notification rules

Open **Settings → Notification rules**, choose a portal, then select **View
rules**. For each notice and channel you can choose **Required**, **On by
default**, **Off by default**, or **Not offered**, then save that channel.
The change affects only your current school and selected portal.

Account safety notices, urgent school notices and parents' absence alerts stay
required. People can change optional choices in their app's **Notifications →
Settings**. A required channel shows a lock and its reason. A notice belonging
to a switched-off module is unavailable.

Use the urgent option on the notification form for time-sensitive school
responsibilities. It makes the notice required. Normal school notices respect
each recipient's optional choices. Existing inbox history remains available.
