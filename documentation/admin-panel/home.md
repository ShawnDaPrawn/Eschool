# Home and the setup checklist

Home is the first page after you sign in. At the top is a button for the job people do most, **Add a learner**, then the setup checklist, today's numbers and the dashboard.

## Get your school ready

For a new school, Home shows seven steps that take it from installed to ready for learners. Each step says what it needs and has a **Do this now** button that opens the page that does it.

| Step | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| School profile | The school's name, email, phone and address are filled in (**Settings > School profile and settings**). |
| School year and terms | This year is set up, has not ended, and has at least one term (**Academics > School year > Terms**). |
| Grades and homerooms | There is at least one grade with a homeroom, such as Grade 5 A. |
| Subjects | There are subjects and the grades have their subjects for this year (**Subjects per grade**). The step shows how many grades have them. |
| Teachers and staff | There are teachers and the homerooms have their homeroom teachers for this year. The step shows how many do. |
| Learners and parents | Learners are enrolled in this year, with their parents. |
| Fees | There are fee types and the grades have their fees for this year. The step shows how many do. |

The steps are worked out from what is in the panel, so a step turns to **Done** as soon as the page behind it has something in it for this year (check the counts it shows to see whether everything is covered); there is nothing to tick off by hand. When every step is done, Home says **Your school is ready for learners** and folds the list into one line; select that line to see the steps again.

If the school year has ended, the year step says so and links to [Start the new school year](./new-year.md).

The checklist only appears for people who can do at least one of the steps. Teachers do not see it.

## Today

Below the checklist are four numbers, each shown only to people allowed to see it, and each opening its list when you select it:

- **Learners this year**: learners enrolled in the school year chosen in the header.
- **Staff present today**: from today's staff register, or a note that it has not been taken.
- **Registers taken today**: homerooms whose attendance has been taken today, and how many are still to do.
- **Notices this year**: notices for the school year, and how many were sent in the last seven days.

The numbers follow the school year chosen in the header (see [School years](./session-year.md)). The dashboard widgets below them are the same as before.
