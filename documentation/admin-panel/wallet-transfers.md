# Parent wallet transfers

A verified parent can move their own available wallet funds to a child for whom
they currently have approving guardian authority. Wallets work independently of
school fees. Open Wallets, select the child, enter the amount, review the direction
and confirm. Your wallet balances and transfer receipt refresh after success.

Returning a child's balance requires a school decision. In **Money → Wallets**,
authorized Finance policy managers can disable returns or allow any current
approving guardian to return available funds to their own wallet. Review how that
rule affects separated families before enabling it. Previously posted movements
remain in the ledger when policy changes.

If a request was interrupted, reopen Wallets and choose **Resolve interrupted wallet
request**. The app checks the original outcome before allowing another movement.
A recorded request appears in your balances and transfer history; an unrecorded
request is safely closed so it cannot arrive late. No bank payment is executed here.
