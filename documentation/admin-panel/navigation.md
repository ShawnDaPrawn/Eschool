# Finding your way around

The menu on the left is arranged by the job you want to do, not by how the data is stored. Every page you could open before is still there, at the same web address; only its place in the menu and some names have changed.

## The eight sections

| Section | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See what the school still has to set up, today's numbers and the dashboard. |
| **People** | Learners (add, import, applications, homeroom placement, roll numbers, ID cards, passwords), parents, teachers and staff, and staff leave. |
| **Academics** | School years and terms, starting the new school year, promoting learners, holidays, grades and homerooms, subjects, who teaches what, the timetable, attendance, lessons and assignments, exams and results, online exams. |
| **Communication** | Notices, push notifications, app banners and the public website's content. |
| **Money** | Fee types, fees per grade, fees paid, the payment log and the fee and payment setup. |
| **Activities** | Events, photos and videos. |
| **Wellbeing** | Learners' leave requests. |
| **Settings** | The school profile, app settings, languages, extra profile fields, ID card design, roles and permissions, email and notifications, and the policies and pages the apps show. |

You only see the sections and pages your role allows. A teacher, for example, sees their learners, registers, lessons, marks, notices and leave, and no Money or Settings.

## Using the menu

- Select a section to open it; the section of the page you are on is already open, and the current page is highlighted.
- The button at the top left hides the menu to give the page more room; select it again to bring the menu back.
- On a phone or a narrow window the menu opens from the button at the top left and closes with the cross, by selecting outside it, or with Escape. The school year switcher is at the top of the menu there.

## Using a keyboard

- Press Tab once on any page to show **Skip to content**, then Enter to jump past the menu to the page itself.
- Tab moves through the menu; Enter or Space opens and closes a section.
- Press `/` or Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) to go straight to the search box.

The panel works at a window width of 1024 pixels and wider without scrolling sideways.
